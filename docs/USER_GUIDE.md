# HueRoom User Guide

1. Open the deployed application and create an account, or sign in.
2. Choose **Upload room photo** and select a photo you own or have permission to use. Use JPG, PNG or WEBP under 8 MB.
3. Click around the corners of the wall you want to recolor. Use at least three points. Double-click to finish the polygon.
4. Choose a shade from the palette and adjust opacity. Click **Preview shade** or **Apply color**.
5. Use **Undo point** or **Clear selection** to correct the outline. Download the preview as a PNG.
6. Sign in to save the design under **My Designs**.

Only upload room images you own or are authorized to use. Avoid photos containing private or sensitive information. Digital previews are approximate; test a physical paint sample before purchasing paint.

## Automatic AI wall detection
1. Open Visualizer Studio and upload a clear room photo.
2. Select **Detect walls with AI** below the room canvas.
3. Wait for the model to load and identify the wall region. On first use, model download can take some time and requires internet access.
4. Select a paint shade and adjust opacity to preview it on the detected wall.
5. If the detected area is incomplete or includes unwanted regions, clear the selection and use the manual polygon tool.

AI segmentation is an approximate guide. Review the result before saving; lighting, furniture, windows, wall texture and image quality can affect detection.
